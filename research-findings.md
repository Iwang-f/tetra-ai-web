## Temuan Per Situs

### Synthesia.io
- **Hero:** "All-in-one AI Video platform for business" — category claim + target audience (8 kata)
- **Subheadline:** "Save up to 90% of time and cost on video production" — quantified ROI in subheadline
- **CTA:** "Get started for FREE" + "Book demo" — dual CTA (PLG + enterprise)
- **Social proof:** Logo strip immediately below hero; "Trusted by 50,000+ teams"; "90% of Fortune 100"
- **Pricing:** Tidak ditampilkan di homepage
- **Trust signals:** SOC2 Type II, ISO 42001, GDPR, 2,000+ G2 reviews 4.7/5
- **Process:** 5 langkah (Create → Edit → Collaborate → Translate → Publish), UI screenshots per step
- **Testimonials:** Woven into feature sections dengan before/after contrast ("100 hours → 10 minutes")

### Pictory.ai
- **Hero:** "Generate Videos in Minutes from Text" — speed + input method (7 kata)
- **CTA micro-copy:** "* No credit card required" tepat di bawah CTA button
- **Social proof:** "Loved by Creators Worldwide | Rated 4.7/5 on G2" di atas hero fold
- **Logo strip:** G2, Capterra, Feefo, Software Advice badges (multi-platform review trust)
- **Client count:** "Trusted by over 20,000 companies of all sizes" — framing "of all sizes" inklusif
- **Pricing:** Tidak di homepage; 3 entry points di nav
- **Process:** "4 Easy Steps" — kata "Easy" sebagai conversion copy
- **Mobile:** Sticky nav CTA "Get Started Now ->"

### InVideo.io
- **Hero:** "Create Videos Without Limits" — 5 kata, aspiration-led
- **CTA framing:** "Try invideo" — "Try" = low-commitment signal
- **Social proof:** "50M+ users across 190 countries" + "8M+ videos/month" (volume metrics)
- **Testimonials:** Semua 4 quote menggunakan before/after — "I used to [pain] — now [result]"
- **Pricing:** Ditampilkan di homepage (satu-satunya dari 8 situs)
- **Trust:** AI model partnerships: Google Veo, Sora 2, Kling, ElevenLabs, iStock
- **Mobile:** App Store + Google Play badges sebagai conversion path

### Runway ML
- **Hero:** "Building AI to Simulate the World" — mission statement, bukan product description
- **Social proof:** NVIDIA, Lionsgate, UCLA — name-drop logos mid-page
- **CTA:** "Try Runway" / "Try now" — low-commitment
- **Process:** Tidak ada; diganti product showcase grid
- **Pricing:** Tidak di homepage

### Waymark.com
- **Hero:** "AI Video Ad Creative That Drives Revenue" — satu-satunya yang eksplisit sebut "Revenue" di H1
- **Subheadline:** "AI video ads. Made in minutes. Built to perform." — staccato 3 fragments
- **Social proof:** Logo carousel (15+) LANGSUNG di bawah hero + stats section: 42% conversion boost, 70% email engagement, 94% production time savings, 100% platform optimization
- **CTA:** "Contact Sales" — enterprise gate, muncul 4x
- **Process:** 5-step accordion — paling eksplisit dari semua situs
- **Trust:** Named executive testimonial dengan title dan perusahaan, physical address

### Maven.com
- **Hero:** "Unlock your career growth" — 4 kata, outcome promise
- **Trust:** Instructor credentials: "Ex-Airbnb", "Ex-Google", "PhD in ML" — pedigree sebagai trust
- **CTA:** Direct links ke course (bukan generic "Sign Up")
- **Process:** 3 format cards (Cohort / Workshop / Free Lessons), bukan sequential steps

### LearnPrompting.org
- **Hero:** "Learn, Apply, & Get Certified in AI" — 3-part action promise
- **Social proof:** "3,000,000+ people" prominently displayed; "Join more than 3 million learners"
- **CTA:** "Start Learning For Free" — muncul 3x di halaman (high repetition pattern)
- **CTA secondary:** "For Businesses" sebagai enterprise separator

### DeepLearning.AI
- **Hero:** "AI is the new electricity. You are the spark." — metaphor + empowerment (10 kata)
- **Authority signal:** Andrew Ng name dalam subheadline sendiri (founder-as-brand)
- **Social proof:** "Join over 7 million people" mid-page
- **Trust:** Free downloadable books sebagai expertise signal tanpa paywall

## 5 Pola Dominan (dipakai 3+ kompetitor)

1. **Friction-removal micro-copy di bawah CTA** — Pictory, Synthesia, LearnPrompting, InVideo
2. **Before/after contrast di testimonials** — InVideo (×4), Synthesia case studies, Waymark
3. **Partner/tool logo strip sebagai social proof, bukan capability list** — Synthesia, Pictory, InVideo, Waymark, Runway
4. **Time-to-value label per process step** — Waymark (5-step), Pictory ("4 Easy Steps"), InVideo
5. **Quantified outcome stat prominent dekat final CTA** — Waymark (42%/94%), Synthesia (90%), Pictory ("minutes")
